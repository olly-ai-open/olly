The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers this repository's code and documentation.

reserved:

- `apps/web/public/olly-mascot.png` (the mascot)
- `apps/web/public/favicon.ico`, `build/icon.icns`, `build/icon.ico`, and
  `build/icon.png` (the icons)

What you may do:

- Build and run this project, modified or not, for your own use with the
  name, mascot, and icons left in place.
- Use the name to refer to this project, for example "a fork of Olly".

What you may not do:

- Distribute a build, modified or not and under any name, that includes the
  mascot or icons, or that calls itself Olly. Before you distribute one,
  replace them with your own name, mascot, and icons. The
  [build guide](docs/desktop-build.md#forks-replace-the-name-mascot-and-icons)
  lists where each one is used.
